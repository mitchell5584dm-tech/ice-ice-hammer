Good to know

Before you press play

A few quick answers about creating and keeping your tracks.

Does it sing my lyrics?

Choose a model that supports vocals, add your lyrics, and generate a take. Instrumental models are labeled in the studio.

Can I make another version?

Yes. Reuse a finished track's settings or remix it to create another take with a fresh variation.

Can I work with the result?

Download finished tracks, split supported tracks into stems, and open them in the Studio mixer for balancing and WAV export.

Where do I start?

Open the Studio, create an account or sign in, then start with lyrics, a style, or an instrumental idea.
